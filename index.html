About THOP TV

Your Personal Movie & TV Guide

THOP TV was built for people who love cinema and television but hate cluttered, subscription-heavy apps. We believe discovering great content should be simple, fast, and free.

Powered by The Movie Database (TMDB), our app pulls real-time data on millions of movies and TV shows — ratings, descriptions, genres, seasons, and official trailers — all beautifully presented in a clean white-and-red interface you'll love using every day.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2025

1. Introduction

Welcome to THOP TV ("we," "our," or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use the THOP TV Android application. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our practices.

2. Information We Collect

THOP TV does not collect any personally identifiable information directly. The app does not require account registration or sign-in. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or location data.

Our app may transmit anonymous, non-personal usage data to third-party advertising partners for the purpose of serving relevant advertisements. This data does not identify you personally.

3. Third-Party Services

THOP TV uses the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policies:

The Movie Database (TMDB) — We use the TMDB API to fetch movie and TV show data including posters, descriptions, ratings, and trailers. Visit TMDB Privacy Policy.

YouTube — Trailers open in the YouTube app or browser. Visit Google Privacy Policy.

Google AdMob — We may display ads through Google AdMob which uses device identifiers. Visit Google Privacy Policy.

Meta Audience Network — Ads may be served through Meta's network. Visit Meta Privacy Policy.

AppLovin — Ads may be served through AppLovin MAX. Visit AppLovin Privacy Policy.

Unity Ads — Ads may be served through Unity Ads. Visit Unity Privacy Policy.

4. Advertising

THOP TV is free to use and is supported by advertising. Our ad partners may use cookies, device identifiers, and similar technologies to serve personalized advertisements based on your interests. You can opt out of personalized advertising through your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization).

5. Children's Privacy

THOP TV is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data through the app, please contact us and we will take steps to remove it.

6. Data Security

Since we do not store any personal data on our servers, there is minimal security risk from our side. However, we encourage you to keep your device software up to date and use appropriate security measures on your device.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

8. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or the app, please reach out through the Google Play Store listing for THOP TV.
